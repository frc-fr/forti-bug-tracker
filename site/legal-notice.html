Data origin

The data is automatically extracted from the FortiOS Release Notes published by Fortinet. Each bug links to the matching official note. Recommended releases and the end of engineering support come from the "Recommended release for FortiOS" article of the Fortinet community. A few bugs and versions carry a manual annotation, with a link to the Fortinet article it comes from. Support dates of the FortiOS branches come from endoflife.date: this community source does not come from Fortinet.
